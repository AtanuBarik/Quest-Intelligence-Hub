How answers are generated

Free local document agent

The browser parses your files, retrieves the most relevant passages, then chooses local QA or local synthesis depending on the question.

  • No paid ChatGPT or Microsoft Copilot API is required.
  • Uploaded project files stay in the browser.
  • Direct questions use a local extractive QA model.
  • Summaries and comparisons use a small local synthesis model with grounding checks.
  • PDF pages, PowerPoint slides, and Excel sheets are retained as source locations.
  • Legacy .doc and .ppt files should be saved as .docx or .pptx before upload.
