How answers are generated

Local AI over your uploaded files

The browser reads your files, retrieves the most relevant passages, and runs a free question-answering model locally over those passages.

  • No paid API key is required.
  • Your uploaded project files stay in the browser.
  • The first question downloads the free local QA model; later questions reuse the browser cache.
  • Answers include supporting file, PDF page, or spreadsheet sheet citations where available.
  • If the files do not support the answer, Insights Copilot does not use outside knowledge.
